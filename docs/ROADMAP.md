# OpenGeoResearch Roadmap

## Vision

OpenGeoResearch aims to become the leading open-source platform for research data collection, geospatial analytics, and AI-assisted research insights.

The roadmap is organized into milestone-based releases that progressively expand the platform's capabilities while maintaining research integrity, scalability, and ease of use.

---

# Current Status

Phase: Planning

Status: Pre-Development

Version Target: v1.0.0

---

# Guiding Principles

1. Research-first design
2. GIS-native architecture
3. Open-source community driven
4. Reproducible science
5. AI-assisted, not AI-dependent
6. Modular and extensible
