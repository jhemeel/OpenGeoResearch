# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the OpenGeoResearch community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Visible or invisible disability
- Ethnicity
- Sex characteristics
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Education
- Socio-economic status
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing respect toward other contributors and users

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action
- Protecting contributors from harassment and abuse
- Maintaining a welcoming environment

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Discussions
- Pull requests
- Issue trackers
- Documentation
- Community communication channels

It also applies when an individual is officially representing OpenGeoResearch in public spaces.

Examples include:

- Using an official project email address
- Posting via official social media accounts
- Acting as a designated project representative

---

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** Private written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate.

---

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** Repeated inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** Warning with clear expectations for future behavior.

---

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** Serious violation.

**Consequence:** Temporary suspension from community interaction.

---

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Sustained or severe misconduct.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from project participation.
